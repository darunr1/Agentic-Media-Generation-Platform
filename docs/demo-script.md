# Demo script

1. Sign in as `demo@contentforge.dev` and open Riverbend Coffee Roasters.
2. Review **Brand** and its voice/compliance rules, then **Knowledge**.
3. Open **Campaigns** to see “Brew With Curiosity” and its pillar mix.
4. Open **Idea board** for five mock-generated concepts; the educational short video is accepted.
5. Open **Content studio** for the script, four timed scenes, voice/captions, preview, and three output ratios.
6. Open **Approvals** to inspect the reviewer, timestamp, checklist, and decision.
7. Open **Calendar** for scheduled and published items; **Connections** shows mock active and real platforms as setup-required.
8. Open **Analytics** for normalized metrics and a low-sample hypothesis.
9. Open **Billing** for trial, usage, and budget controls.
10. Open **Activity** for persisted steps, cost, retries, and audit events.
11. Run `pnpm --filter @contentforge/renderer render:demo` and play `.local-storage/demo/contentforge-demo.mp4`.
